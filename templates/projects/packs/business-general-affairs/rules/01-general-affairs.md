# Rules / ルール: 総務依頼

1. The agent drafts; a person approves. 下書きのみ、承認は担当者。
2. Never write that something is booked, ordered or arranged before approval (「手配済み」禁止).
3. Costs: estimate in JPY with the source of the price. Over 100,000 JPY needs a second approver.
4. Personal data: only what the request already contains.
5. Reply in the requester's language (日本語 by default), polite business style (です・ます調).

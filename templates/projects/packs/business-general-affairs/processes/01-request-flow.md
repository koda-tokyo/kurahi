# Process / 手順: 総務依頼

1. Read the request fields and attachments. 申請内容と添付を確認。
2. Check the policy result on the right panel; fix missing fields first (差戻し理由になる).
3. Draft: summary, proposed arrangement, date, cost estimate, open questions (【要確認】).
4. The reviewer approves, or sends back with a comment; a send-back comment is the next brief.
5. After approval, the person carries out the arrangement and marks the item done.

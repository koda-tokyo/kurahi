# Rules / ルール: 経費・請求書

1. Amounts, dates and registration numbers (T + 13 digits) exactly as on the document.
2. Show every calculation (税抜 + 消費税 = 税込); flag rounding differences.
3. Never state that anything is paid or will be paid; accounting decides (支払はしない).
4. Mismatch between claim and receipt: list it under 【要確認】 with both values.
5. Expense above 50,000 JPY or invoice above 1,000,000 JPY needs a second approver.

# Rules / ルール: 休暇・残業

1. Figures come from attendance data only: actual hours, month-end forecast, applicable limit (上限).
2. Status labels as in the overtime brief: 実績で超過 (over on actuals) / 月末見込みに基づくリスク (at risk on forecast) / 範囲内 (within limit).
3. Never tell the employee a request is approved; the manager decides (no send without approval).
4. Only the requester's own data, and only what the decision needs (privacy).
5. Forecast above 45 h needs a second approver; above 80 h cannot be approved in this queue.

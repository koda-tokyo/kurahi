# Process / 手順: 残業申請の確認

1. Read employeeId, date, hours, reason, monthToDateHours and forecastMonthHours.
2. Compare with the applicable limit (default 45 h/month, 360 h/year; special clause per 36協定).
3. Draft a note for the approver: figures table (実績 / 見込み / 上限 / 状態), the reason, risks.
4. Draft the reply to the employee, clearly marked as pending the manager's decision.
5. Leave: confirm balance (残日数) from the attendance export, dates, cover arrangements.

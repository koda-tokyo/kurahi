# Department workflow / 部門業務

1. Choose the relevant departmental queue. 適切な部門キューを選択。
2. Capture requester, description, source documents, owner and an explicit due date when provided. Missing dates remain unset.
3. Prepare an evidence-backed draft. 資料に基づいて下書きを作成。
4. Submit for person review under the project's policy. 人によるレビューに提出。
5. Record only verified outcomes. Do not equate approval with payment, delivery or completion of an external action.

The company-wide template accepts request, case and document records. Specialised invoice, expense, leave and purchasing rules remain available in their dedicated templates. This starter policy is not a substitute for company-specific controls.

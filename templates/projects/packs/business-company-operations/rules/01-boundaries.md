# Working boundaries / 業務の境界

Agents prepare drafts; people approve, return or cancel. エージェントは下書きを作成し、人が承認・差戻し・取消します。
Use only supplied facts and documents. Mark unknowns as to confirm / 要確認.
Do not send messages, make payments, book appointments, change accounts or claim an external action occurred. These queues track requests and reviews; they do not provide external integrations.
Department labels organise work, not permissions. The starter policy uses access fallback none. Configure role membership and access rules before adding confidential HR, finance or legal records. Privileged project owners retain their existing access.
申請と添付資料のみを根拠とし、外部システムの処理を実行済みと記載しないでください。部門名は表示用です。機密情報の登録前にアクセス権を設定してください。

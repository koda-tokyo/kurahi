# Reporting boundaries / 報告の境界

- Preserve source Project, query scope, reader, timestamp and evidence references for every
  figure. Never combine overlapping snapshots by adding totals. A department is navigation,
  not authorization. Cross-Project reporting is a manual, permission-checked comparison.
  数値ごとに元プロジェクト・条件・閲覧者・時刻・証跡を保持。重複集計を加算しない。
- Missing/denied/unavailable evidence stays unknown; no invented values or zero defaults.
  The source may change between reads. Label snapshots taken at different times and avoid
  implying a transactionally consistent report.
  欠落・権限不足・取得不可は不明。取得時刻の違いを明記し、同一時点の保証はしない。
- An approved case proves a decision, not delivery, receipt, payroll, payment or reconciliation.
  Provisional quotations remain provisional even when released with an override.
  承認は判断の証拠で、納品・受領・給与・支払・照合完了ではない。暫定見積は暫定のまま。
- Estimated time saved is a configured estimate, not measured productivity. Record the
  baseline, sample size, period and measurement method before claiming measured improvement.
  推定削減時間は実測効果ではない。実測には基準・件数・期間・測定方法を明記。
- Worksheets are manual instructions, not executable validation, access policy or a dashboard.
  Keep source exports and worksheets read-only to agents; drafts go only to output/ and need
  human review. People handle report corrections, withdrawal and authorized distribution.
  ひな型は実行される検証・権限設定・画面ではない。下書きはoutput/、修正・撤回・配布は人が行う。

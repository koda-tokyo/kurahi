# Measured outcomes / 実測結果

Manual evidence worksheet. Unknown until independently supported; approval is not completion.
証拠の手動記入表。裏付けがなければ不明。承認は実施完了ではない。

- Owner, reviewer and authorized audience / 担当・確認者・共有先:
- Period, scope and sample selection / 期間・範囲・対象選択:
- Baseline method, period and sample size / 基準の測定方法・期間・件数:
- Current method, period and sample size / 現在の測定方法・期間・件数:
- Missing/excluded/failed cases / 欠落・除外・失敗案件:

No automated downstream verification or business outcome calculation is provided. A verified
artifact hash proves which artifact was reviewed, not that an order was placed or paid.

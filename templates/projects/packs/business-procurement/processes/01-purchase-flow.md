# Process / 手順: 購買申請

1. Read the request and every quote. 見積書をすべて確認。
2. Comparison table, recommended vendor with the reason, risks (納期, 支払条件).
3. Draft the order proposal and, if asked, the vendor email (送信しない).
4. Budget holder approves or sends back; after approval a person places the order and marks done.

# Process: industrial modernization

1. **Discover:** inventory code, jobs, data, interfaces, equipment, networks, runbooks, owners and evidence freshness.
2. **Map:** connect calls, data flows, job chains, files, messages, PLC tags, schedules and trust boundaries. Cite facts; mark unresolved links unverified.
3. **Assess:** separate business, safety, cyber, operations and data risks; record criticality and recovery needs.
4. **Design:** preserve observable behavior; define seams, contracts, coexistence, reconciliation and rollback in small migration waves.
5. **Transform:** write reviewable drafts only in allowed folders and link them to source evidence.
6. **Validate:** record environment, inputs, expected and actual results for equivalence, reconciliation, performance, recovery and security tests.
7. **Approve:** present impact, residual risk, evidence and rollback; require on-screen approval and retain the final Artifact hash.

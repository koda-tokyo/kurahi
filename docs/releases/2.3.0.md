# Kurahi 2.3.0

Also available in [Japanese](/ja/releases/2.3.0).

Kurahi 2.3.0 turns the Business Edition into functional packs over the systems a company already
uses, adds quotations and purchasing, and lets Autopilot reason with a model API or a model on the
company's own machine. Kurahi is built by the KODA SōKEN team. The complete list of changes is in
the [changelog](https://github.com/koda-tokyo/kurahi/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

With 2.3.0 you can:

- **Prepare quotations from your own price list.** A quotation request becomes a case with a quotation
  in Word and Excel. Kurahi computes every amount and the consumption tax per rate, as Japan's qualified invoice system requires;
  items the price list cannot price are marked "to confirm", and a quotation with open items can only be
  released as a provisional estimate. Sending is always a person's action.
- **Compare supplier quotes and draft purchase orders.** Keep a case's supplier quotes (from CSV or
  Excel sheets), compare them, record a person's choice and draft each chosen supplier's purchase order.
- **Run work that spans departments.** A linked case plans steps in several departments, tracks
  dependencies and escalates stuck cases; a pack's worksheet can start one from the console.
- **Keep data for as long as you must, and no longer.** Retention rules with signed manifests,
  legal holds, eDiscovery export, a My Number guard and data subject requests.
- **Run Autopilot without a command-line model.** Routines can reason through any
  OpenAI-compatible model API or a local Ollama model; content need not leave your network.

## What is new

### Business Edition

- **Quotations:** price lists in CSV (UTF-8 or Shift_JIS) or Excel (.xlsx); tax per rate
  rounded once per document; revisions (R2, R3 …) name the version they supersede; approval is bound
  to the exact files; a price list older than the company's limit needs a person's confirmation.
- **Purchasing:** supplier quote comparison (up to 20 suppliers and 200 items), a recorded supplier
  choice (passing over the lowest quote needs a reason) and purchase-order drafts that keep the sheet
  they came from.
- **Functional packs:** Sales quotation, Employee requests, People operations, Contracts and
  obligations and Management oversight, each with forms, routes, checks and Japanese and English
  guidance. Each pack's page lists what it does not include.
- **Cases across departments, hand-offs and notifications:** linked cases, hand-off tracking from
  approval to closure (only a person attests that a payment settled), in-app notifications, and
  withdrawal of a delegation before the delegate uses it.
- **Document previews** of attached PDF and Office files, and faster work queues for large volumes.

### Autopilot

- **`backend: api`:** a routine names `<provider>/<model>` (for example `sakura/…`, a Qwen endpoint
  or `ollama/<model>`). The request carries no tools; replies with tool calls, cut off at the token
  limit, filtered or refused are discarded with the reason. HTTPS is required except for a model on
  the same machine or a private network.
- **Local models:** a local Ollama model is given a context that fits the gathered content, and an
  answer whose prompt filled the context is discarded. A call waits as long as the routine's
  `timeoutSeconds` allows (up to 900 s).
- A routines file naming a reasoning backend the engine cannot run is refused when it is loaded.

### Data governance

- `governance` in the configuration: retention per data class with signed sweep manifests, legal
  holds, minimum retention for audit and evidence, eDiscovery export, personal-data handling
  (My Number and other identifiers) and data subject requests (APPI).

### Kurahi Desktop

- Desktop 0.5.0 is unchanged and works with 2.3.0; its installers are attached to this release.

## Fixed

- Project files reached through a symlinked folder (macOS `/var`, `/tmp`) are accepted when their
  real path is inside the Project.
- Price lists refuse oversized cells and duplicated item codes instead of guessing.

## Upgrade from 2.2.0

`kurahi update` (or `install.sh` / `install.ps1` with `--version 2.3.0`) installs 2.3.0 as usual;
see [Updating](/install/updating). A 2.2.0 configuration loads unchanged; everything new is off
until you configure it. Standard and enterprise packages are published as in 2.2.0, and optional
plugins as `kurahi-<id>-2.3.0.tgz` with a `.sha256`.

## Roll back

`install.sh --version 2.2.0` (Windows: `install.ps1 -Version 2.2.0`) installs 2.2.0 again; see
[Updating](/install/updating#rollback). Before you start 2.2.0:

- **Remove `governance`** from the configuration file: 2.2.0 does not accept keys it does not
  know and would not use the file.
- Routines with `reasoner: { backend: api }` do not run on 2.2.0; switch them to `codex-cli` or
  `claude-cli`, or pause them.
- 2.2.0 does not know work queue rules with `overrideOutcome` or the 2.3.0 packs and may refuse
  those policy files; pause those Projects first. Quotations, linked cases and hand-offs created by
  2.3.0 stay on disk, but 2.2.0 has no screens or tools for them.

## Notes

- Kurahi is self-hosted: your servers and any model provider you choose are separate from Kurahi.
- Checksums for every asset are in `SHA256SUMS.txt` and `SHA256SUMS-desktop.txt`.

# Kurahi Scholars

Also available in [Japanese](/ja/scholars).

**Applications will open soon.** This page describes the programme so that researchers and teachers
can plan for it. There is nothing to apply for yet, and the programme and its terms may change
before applications open.

Kurahi Scholars is the academic [Edition](/editions) of Kurahi. It is free for non-commercial
research and teaching at universities and research institutes, in any country. The only screening
is what the law requires of KODA SōKEN, such as sanctions and export-control rules. At launch the
programme is for universities and research institutes; schools come later.

## Research and Education workspaces (coming)

Scholars has two workspaces, chosen by role:

| Workspace | For         | Contents                                                         | Status |
| --------- | ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Research  | Researchers | Studies, data, experiments, literature, provenance, publications | Coming |
| Education | Teachers    | Courses, lessons, assignments, tutor, portfolio, progress        | Coming |

The workspaces are defined in the shell but their screens are not built yet. The role switch,
notebook and cluster integration, classroom sandboxes and literature connectors are also planned,
not available.

## What works today

Through Kurahi Projects, without the dedicated workspace screens:

- the **Research & academic** project template with three [autopilot](/autopilot#scholars)
  routines over free literature APIs ([Scholar sources](/connectors/scholar-sources): arXiv,
  OpenAlex, Crossref, PubMed; no keys): a weekly literature watch from your research profile with
  a Word digest, a citation check of your drafts, and grant deadline reminders. Nothing is ever
  submitted. The corpus is read-only and every claim cites a
  source file;
- the **Education** template for teachers' lesson planning, without learner personal data;
- [evidence bundles](/evidence): a signed record of every agent job, verified offline;
- the hash-chained audit log, [policy as code](/policy) and human approvals;
- the signed [AI inventory](/inventory) of what ran;
- [memory provenance](/security/memory-provenance) and the [context guard](/context-guard);
- the [Workflow Evaluator](/eval): acceptance specs, offline replay, signed scorecards;
- the [legacy analyser](/tools/legacy-analyzer) for COBOL/JCL, Java and SQL DDL, for software
  engineering research.

Kurahi runs on the institution's or the scholar's own machines and sends no telemetry to KODA SōKEN
([Privacy](/security/privacy)). The scholar chooses the model, hosted or local, and pays for model
usage and compute. Builds are not code-signed yet.

## Tracks

| Track        | For                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Scholar      | One researcher, teacher or supervised student. Independent researchers with a reference letter from a researcher at a recognised institution         |
| Research Lab | A group or a course under one principal investigator or teacher: one shared policy, shared audit and evidence; up to 25 seats; the institution signs |
| Fellow       | Selected work that advances open, inspectable agent research or teaching, chosen by a review of the proposal. Details will follow                    |

## Freedom to publish

- Scholars may publish anything they learn with Kurahi or about Kurahi, including benchmarks,
  failures and negative results, without asking KODA SōKEN first and without our review.
- The one exception is a security vulnerability that could put other users at risk: report it first
  through the process in `SECURITY.md`. The coordinated-disclosure window is at most 90 days and
  covers only the details needed to exploit the issue.
- Data, code, papers and results belong to the scholar.

## Not covered

- Corporate research and development, including a company's own laboratory (a paid Research pack is
  planned for that).
- Work for a company's product, service or internal operations.
- Model usage, compute, hosting, and support with a response-time commitment.

Scholars does not replace an institution's ethics or data-protection approval.

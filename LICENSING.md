## Plans (freemium — decision: KODA, 2026-10-25)

Security is never a paid extra: single sign-on (OIDC, SAML), SCIM provisioning and roles are part of
the runtime in every plan.

| Plan           | Price             | Includes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Free**       | free              | the Open and Source-available layers above, self-hosted: editions (Business, Industrial, Scholars), work queue, connectors, Office documents, SSO / SCIM / roles, attachment scanning, autopilot at L0–L1 (observe and prepare)                                                                                                                                               |
| **Enterprise** | written agreement | everything in Free, plus: digital staff (autopilot routines at L2–L3 with standing approvals, per digital staff member), the data governance suite with assurance (retention, legal hold, eDiscovery export), air-gap and offline updates, high availability, multi-organisation admin, certification pack (ISMS / NISC mapping), support with an SLA in Japanese and English |

Prices are set per agreement. Licence keys and in-product plan enforcement arrive with the
licensing work on the enterprise roadmap; until then the Enterprise plan is a contract and support
commitment, and nothing in the Free plan is time-limited.

Third-party components keep their own licenses and notices (see `NOTICE`). Components with copyleft dependencies are not part of the default package, prebuilt bundles or installers: the WhatsApp channel (its `libsignal` dependency is GPL-3.0) is an optional plugin installed separately (`docs/channels/whatsapp.md`). The release SBOM (`pnpm sbom`) shows the default runtime without it. Where this repository derives from upstream open-source work, the upstream license and attribution are retained in the relevant files; KODA's own contributions and the whole are licensed as above.

Trademarks: "Kurahi", "KODA", "KODA Kenkō" and the KODA marks are not licensed under any of the above and may not be used to identify forks or derived products (see `TRADEMARKS.md`).

Contributions: external contributions require the Contributor License Agreement in `CLA.md`.

Industrial agents ("Kurahi", クラヒ — the commercial resident agents, decided 2026-10-13): agents built on this platform receive identity, memory and policy from the proprietary identity engine but never KODA's own identity files. KODA's own agents are not a product.
